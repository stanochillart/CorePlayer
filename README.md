# CorePlayer

### Your music. Your device. No cloud.

**[Download](https://github.com/stanochillart/CorePlayer/releases/download/v1.2/CorePlayer-1.2.apk)** · Android 9+ · no account · no ads

---

Morning. Headphones on the table. The stream waits for a network. Then an account. Then whether the subscription still pays. The playlist you built for a year sits behind a door with no key.

One floor down, the files are still in the phone. Things you saved when music still went in a folder. The other half of the collection is on the card.

CorePlayer asks only one thing: where to look.

You add a folder. The music appears. Albums, artists, covers. You close the window. It keeps playing. The files go nowhere.

---

It plays what you already have. Phone, card, folders you choose.

Your playlists. What you added lately. What you play most. Equalizer. A timer if you want to fall asleep to it.

You can control it from the lock screen. The music stays on the device.

---

For someone with a collection of their own. For anyone who does not want to depend on a subscription.

This is not a catalogue of millions of tracks. It will not tell you what to listen to. It will not play anything that is not on the phone.

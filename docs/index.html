CorePlayer

Your music. Your device. No cloud.

Download · Android 9+ · no account · no ads

Morning. Headphones on the table. The stream waits for a network. Then an account. Then whether the subscription still pays. The playlist you built for a year sits behind a door with no key.

One floor down, the files are still in the phone. CorePlayer asks only one thing: where to look.

You add a folder. The music appears. You close the window. It keeps playing. The files go nowhere.

It plays what you already have. Playlists. Equalizer. A timer if you want to fall asleep to it. Control from the lock screen.

This is not a catalogue of millions of tracks. It will not play anything that is not on the phone.
